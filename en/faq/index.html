Frequently asked questions

What is Stayora?

Stayora is hotel management software (a PMS) for small hotels and guesthouses. Your booking calendar, check-in and check-out, rooms and cleaning, guests, rates and reports are all in one place.

Who is Stayora for?

Small hotels, guesthouses and boutique hotels, where the owner often works the front desk too. Our first pilot hotel has 10 rooms.

How much does it cost?

Stayora is free during the pilot. No card and no contract. We’ll tell you well in advance before anything costs money.

How do I get access?

Fill in the “Request access” form. During the pilot we approve each hotel personally and email you. Then you sign up in the web app, and the setup guide creates your hotel.

Does it connect to Booking.com and Airbnb?

Yes, through calendar import (iCal). Stayora reads your Booking.com and Airbnb calendars every 15 minutes and the bookings appear in your calendar. Clashes show up in “Needs attention”. It is not two-way sync yet: if you take a direct booking, also close the date on Booking.com or Airbnb.

How does it help avoid double bookings?

The whole team works in the same calendar. You see how many rooms are free on every date, and imported Booking.com and Airbnb bookings sit next to your direct ones.

Can I use Stayora on my phone?

Yes. The web app works in the browser on a computer, tablet or phone. iPhone (TestFlight, then the App Store) and Android (Google Play) apps are coming soon.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Add your team and give each person a role, like front desk, cleaning or manager. Everyone sees only what they need.

Which languages does it support?

Romanian, English, German, French, Italian and Spanish.

Can I take online payments or send SMS?

Not yet. Stayora tracks payments (paid or unpaid), but it doesn’t process card payments and doesn’t send SMS or WhatsApp messages.

Who owns the data?

Your hotel’s data belongs to you. Our privacy policy explains how we protect it.

Still have questions?

Write to us and we’ll reply within one business day.
