Privacy policy

Last updated: [date] · Draft of 30 October 2026

1. Who we are

The website www.stayora.ro and the Stayora app are operated by [Company name], VAT/CUI [number], Trade Register no. [J..], registered at [address], Romania. Privacy contact: [email].

2. Two different roles

Website and forms: for data you send us through this website (for example “Request access” or “Contact”), Stayora is the controller.

The hotel app: for guest data a hotel enters in the app, the hotel is the controller and Stayora is a processor (Art. 28 GDPR). We sign a data processing agreement with every hotel: [link to agreement – to be drafted].

3. What data we process

  • From forms: name, email, hotel name, number of rooms, city, property type and your message.
  • App account: name, email, role in the hotel, login data.
  • Data entered by the hotel: guest names, optional contact details (phone, email), stay dates, room, price and payment status, notes.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and security logs.

4. Why, and on what legal basis

  • To answer your request and approve access – steps before a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) or legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • To provide the app – performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Security and abuse prevention – legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal obligations (for example accounting) – Art. 6(1)(c).

We do not sell data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Who we share it with

We use providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Web3Forms – delivers website form submissions to our email.
  • Vercel – hosts the web app and the server (API).
  • Supabase – database and login, hosted in the EU (Ireland, eu-west-1).
  • GitHub Pages / Cloudflare – hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts – the Inter font; your browser sends your IP address to Google when it loads the font.

Some providers may transfer data outside the EEA (for example to the US). Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. [To be checked for each provider.]

6. How long we keep it

  • Form requests without an account: up to [12] months.
  • Account data: while the account is active, then [X] months.
  • Guest data: as instructed by the hotel; when the contract ends we return or delete it.
  • Accounting records: as long as the law requires.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection. Write to [email]. If you are a hotel guest, please contact the hotel first; we help the hotel respond.

You can complain to the Romanian data protection authority (ANSPDCP), www.dataprotection.ro, or to the authority in your EU country.

8. Cookies

This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies. The web app only uses strictly necessary storage for login.

9. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), role-based access and securely stored passwords. [To be completed with the actual measures.]

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the last update. We email hotels about important changes.
